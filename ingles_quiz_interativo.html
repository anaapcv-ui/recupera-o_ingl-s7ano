Q1
SIMPLE PRESENT
She _____ English every day.
Resposta: B (studies). "Every day" indica Simple Present. Como o sujeito é she (he/she/it), o verbo leva S no final → studies.
Q2
SIMPLE PRESENT — NEGATIVA
My father _____ like to cook.
Resposta: B (doesn't). "My father" = he → usa doesn't + verbo BASE (like). É a Q7 da Prova 1 inteirinha!
Q3
PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Look! The cat _____ on the sofa right now.
Resposta: C (is sleeping). "Right now" → ação acontecendo agora → Present Continuous. Cat = it → IS + verb-ing → is sleeping.
Q4
YES/NO ANSWERS
"Are you doing a test right now?" Resposta correta:
Resposta: B (Yes, I am). Pergunta com are → resposta com am/is/are. Forma curta afirmativa nunca contrai (Yes, I am — não Yes, I'm).
Q5
COMPARATIVO
My brothers and sisters are _____ me.
Resposta: B (older than). "Old" é adjetivo curto (1 sílaba) → comparativo é older than. "More old" está SEMPRE errado!
Q6
SUPERLATIVO
Around the World in Eighty Days is _____ book I read last year.
Resposta: C (the most interesting). Superlativo de adjetivo longo = THE MOST + adjetivo. Não esqueça do "the"!
Q7
COMPARATIVOS — REGRA
Marque a frase CORRETA:
Resposta: C. A única frase 100% correta. A) deveria ser "better"; B) falta "the most"; D) falta "the tallest".
Q8
WAS / WERE
He _____ really happy yesterday.
Resposta: B (was). "Yesterday" = passado. He = singular → was.
Q9
THERE WAS / WERE
In the old room: There _____ four lamps.
Resposta: B (were). "Four lamps" = plural → there were. Olhe o que vem depois: vários = WERE.
Q10
SIMPLE PAST — IRREGULAR
We _____ to Venice last week.
Resposta: C (went). "Last week" = passado. Go é irregular → passado é went.
Q11
SIMPLE PAST — REGULAR
She _____ for the test last night.
Resposta: C (studied). "Study" termina em consoante + Y → tira o Y, põe IED → studied.
Q12
SIMPLE PAST — NEGATIVA
They _____ visit their grandma last Sunday.
Resposta: B (didn't). Negativa do Simple Past = didn't + verbo BASE (visit). Sem -ed!
Q13
PAST CONTINUOUS
They _____ a movie when she called.
Resposta: D (were watching). Ação em andamento no passado → Past Continuous. They = plural → WERE + watching.
Q14
WHEN / WHILE
_____ I was riding my bike to school, I dropped my backpack.
Resposta: B (While). "I was riding" é Past Continuous → combina com WHILE (enquanto). Ação longa em andamento.
Q15
WH-QUESTION
_____ your parents doing when you got home?
Resposta: C (What were). "Doing" = verbo+ing → Past Continuous. Parents = plural → WERE. Pergunta-padrão da Q6 da Prova 2!
