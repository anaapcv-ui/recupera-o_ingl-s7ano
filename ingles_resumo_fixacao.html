ISSUE #1
PRESENT POWERS
SIMPLE PRESENT + PRESENT CONTINUOUS
⚡ SIMPLE PRESENT — rotinas e fatos

Usamos o Simple Present para falar de hábitos, rotinas, fatos e gostos. Coisas que acontecem sempre, geralmente, todos os dias.

always usually often every day on Mondays never
SUJEITO + VERBO (he/she/it +s)
FormaEstruturaExemplo
✅ Afirmativa I/You/We/They + verbo
He/She/It + verbo+s		 I play soccer.
She plays soccer.
❌ Negativa I/You/We/They + don't + verbo
He/She/It + doesn't + verbo		 I don't play soccer.
She doesn't play soccer.
❓ Pergunta Do + I/you/we/they + verbo?
Does + he/she/it + verbo?		 Do you play soccer?
Does she play soccer?
She plays soccer every Saturday. Ela joga futebol todo sábado.
My father doesn't like to cook. Meu pai não gosta de cozinhar.
Do you like to study English? Você gosta de estudar inglês?
POW! ⚠️ Em perguntas e negativas com does/doesn't, o verbo principal NÃO leva S! É: "Does she play?" e não "Does she plays?"
💥 PRESENT CONTINUOUS — agora, neste momento

Usamos o Present Continuous para falar de ações que estão acontecendo agora, neste momento, enquanto eu falo.

now right now at the moment today
SUJEITO + AM/IS/ARE + VERBO-ING
PronomeForma do "BE"Exemplo
Iam (I'm)I am playing. / I'm playing.
He / She / ItisShe is playing. / She's playing.
You / We / TheyareThey are playing. / They're playing.
She is playing soccer now. Ela está jogando futebol agora.
I am studying for my English test. Eu estou estudando para minha prova de inglês.
BAM! 💥 Cuidado com a regra do -ING: verbo terminado em -e, tira o e e põe -ing (write → writing). Verbo curto vogal+consoante, dobra a consoante (sit → sitting).
🦸 SIMPLE PRESENT vs PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Simple Present 🔵Present Continuous 🔴
Sempre / todo dia
I play soccer every Saturday.		 Agora / neste momento
I am playing soccer now.
Hábitos e rotinas Ações em andamento
Fatos: The Earth orbits the Sun. Mudanças: It's getting cold.

A resposta curta usa o mesmo verbo auxiliar da pergunta:

PerguntaSim ✅Não ❌
Do you like pizza?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she dance?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.

ISSUE #2
COMPARE & CONQUER
COMPARATIVOS E SUPERLATIVOS
⚡ COMPARATIVOS — comparar 2 coisas

Para comparar duas coisas, usamos comparativos. A regra muda conforme o tamanho do adjetivo:

TipoRegraExemplo
Adjetivo curto
(1 sílaba)		 adjetivo + -er than tall → taller than
old → older than
young → younger than
Adjetivo longo
(2+ sílabas)		 more + adjetivo + than interesting → more interesting than
difficult → more difficult than
Irregulares
(decoreba!)		 forma própria good → better than
bad → worse than
far → farther than
My brother is taller than me. Meu irmão é mais alto que eu.
Math is more difficult than Art. Matemática é mais difícil que Artes.
POW! 🚫 "More tall" está ERRADO! Tall é curto → vira taller. "Taller than" ✅. Nunca use more com adjetivo curto.
💥 SUPERLATIVOS — o melhor de todos!

Para dizer que algo é o mais de todos, usamos superlativos. Sempre vem com THE antes:

THE + ADJETIVO + EST  |  THE + MOST + ADJETIVO
TipoRegraExemplo
Adjetivo curto the + adjetivo + -est tall → the tallest
old → the oldest
young → the youngest
Adjetivo longo the most + adjetivo interesting → the most interesting
difficult → the most difficult
Irregulares forma própria good → the best
bad → the worst
Dean is the oldest person in the family. Dean é a pessoa mais velha da família.
Around the World in Eighty Days is the most interesting book. A Volta ao Mundo em 80 Dias é o livro mais interessante.
🎯 TABELA RÁPIDA — palavras das provas
AdjetivoComparativoSuperlativo
tall (alto)taller thanthe tallest
old (velho)older thanthe oldest
young (jovem)younger thanthe youngest
short (baixo)shorter thanthe shortest
long (longo)longer thanthe longest
big (grande)bigger thanthe biggest
difficult (difícil)more difficult thanthe most difficult
interesting (interessante)more interesting thanthe most interesting
good (bom) ⚠️better thanthe best
bad (ruim) ⚠️worse thanthe worst
SUPER DICA! Se o adjetivo termina em -y, troca por -i: easy → easier than / the easiest. Se é vogal+consoante, dobra: big → bigger / the biggest.
PRACTICE TIME!
Compare e conquiste! 🏆
1. Sarah's hair is ___ (long) than Karen's. (comparativo)
2. My brothers are ___ (old) than me.
3. History is ___ (interesting) subject in school. (superlativo)
4. Mount Everest is ___ (tall) mountain in the world. (superlativo)
ISSUE #3
WAS & WERE
OS VIAJANTES DO TEMPO ⏰
⏰ WAS / WERE — verbo "ser/estar" no passado

Use WAS ou WERE para dizer que algo era ou estava no passado.

Pronome✅ Afirmativo❌ Negativo
Iwaswasn't
Youwereweren't
He / She / Itwaswasn't
We / You / Theywereweren't
WAS = singular (1 só)  |  WERE = plural (vários) + YOU

❓ Perguntas:

Was he happy yesterday? Ele estava feliz ontem? → Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
Were they at school? Eles estavam na escola? → Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
BAM! 💥 YOU sempre usa WERE, mesmo quando é uma só pessoa! "Were you at home?" e nunca "Was you...".
🦸 THERE WAS / THERE WERE — havia / existia

Para dizer que existia ou havia algo no passado:

THERE WAS = 1 só (singular)  |  THERE WERE = vários (plural)
FormaExemplo
There was (1 só) There was a beautiful sofa in the room.
(Havia um sofá bonito na sala)
There were (vários) There were four lamps in the room.
(Havia quatro luminárias na sala)
There wasn't (negativo singular) There wasn't a desk next to the window.
There weren't (negativo plural) There weren't any books on the bookcase.
There was a beautiful sofa. Havia um sofá bonito.
There were books on the bookcase. Havia livros na estante.
FOCO! 🎯 Olhe o que vem depois: 1 sofa → was. 4 lamps → were. Books (plural) → were. Esse é o segredo da Q9 da prova!
PRACTICE TIME!
Viaje no tempo com sabedoria! ⏰
1. He ___ really happy yesterday.
2. Tom and I ___ at the drugstore at 9 p.m.
3. There ___ a beautiful sofa in the old living room. (1 só sofá)
4. There ___ books on the bookcase. (vários livros)
5. ___ you at school yesterday? (pergunta com YOU)
ISSUE #4
MISSION: PAST
SIMPLE PAST · ações terminadas no passado
🎯 SIMPLE PAST — quando usar?

Para falar de ações que aconteceram e terminaram no passado.

yesterday last week last night last summer two days ago in 2020
FormaEstruturaExemplo
✅ Afirmativo Sujeito + verbo no passado I played soccer.
She went home.
❌ Negativo Sujeito + didn't + verbo BASE I didn't play soccer.
She didn't go home.
❓ Pergunta Did + sujeito + verbo BASE? Did you play soccer?
Did she go home?
POW! 🚫 Em negativas e perguntas com did/didn't, o verbo volta para a forma BASE (sem -ed)! É "Did you play?" e nunca "Did you played?"
📘 VERBOS REGULARES — só adiciona -ED!

A maioria dos verbos forma o passado adicionando -ED. Mas tem regrinhas de spelling:

RegraComo fazExemplos
Regra 1
(maioria)		 + ed play → played
walk → walked
watch → watched
Regra 2
termina em -e		 + d arrive → arrived
like → liked
dance → danced
Regra 3
consoante + y		 tira y, põe ied study → studied
try → tried
cry → cried
Regra 4
vogal + y		 só + ed play → played
enjoy → enjoyed
stay → stayed
Regra 5
vogal + consoante (curto)		 dobra consoante + ed scan → scanned
plan → planned
stop → stopped
⭐ VERBOS IRREGULARES — decoreba!

Não seguem regra. Cada um tem sua forma própria. Precisa memorizar! (Lista completa no fim do material 👇)

I went to school yesterday. go → went (eu fui à escola ontem)
She had breakfast at the hotel. have → had (ela tomou café da manhã no hotel)
They saw many tourists in Rome. see → saw (eles viram muitos turistas em Roma)
DICA DE OURO! 🌟 Os verbos que mais caem em prova: be (was/were), have (had), go (went), do (did), see (saw), get (got), come (came), take (took), make (made), eat (ate), buy (bought).
PRACTICE TIME!
Domine o passado, jovem aprendiz! 🎯
1. I ___ (arrive) in Rome last week.
2. We ___ (go) to Venice yesterday. (irregular!)
3. She ___ (study) for the test last night.
4. They ___ (not visit) their grandma. (forma negativa)
5. ___ you have breakfast this morning? (pergunta no passado)
ISSUE #5
MEANWHILE...
PAST CONTINUOUS · ENQUANTO AS COISAS ACONTECIAM
🎬 PAST CONTINUOUS — ação em andamento no passado

Usamos o Past Continuous (também chamado de Past Progressive) para falar de ações que estavam acontecendo em um momento do passado. Estavam em andamento.

SUJEITO + WAS / WERE + VERBO-ING
PronomeForma do BEExemplo
I / He / She / ItwasI was watching TV.
You / We / TheywereThey were eating popcorn.
They were watching a movie when she called. Eles estavam assistindo um filme quando ela ligou.
I was riding my bike when I dropped my backpack. Eu estava andando de bicicleta quando deixei a mochila cair.
BAM! 💥 Lembrete: YOU, mesmo singular, usa WERE! "What were you doing?" e nunca "What was you doing?"
⚡ WHEN vs WHILE — a dupla dinâmica

As duas significam mais ou menos a mesma coisa, mas se usam diferente:

PalavraCombina comSignificado
WHEN + Simple Past "quando" — ação curta que aconteceu
WHILE + Past Continuous "enquanto" — ação longa em andamento
I was sleeping when the phone rang. Eu estava dormindo quando o telefone tocou.
While I was studying, my brother was playing video games. Enquanto eu estudava, meu irmão estava jogando videogame.
SEGREDO! 🔑 A combinação clássica: WHILE + ação longa (Past Continuous), WHEN + ação curta que interrompe (Simple Past). "While I was eating, the doorbell rang."
🔄 SIMPLE PAST vs PAST CONTINUOUS
Simple PastPast Continuous
Ação rápida e terminada
I called my mom.		 Ação em andamento
I was talking to my mom.
"Eu liguei pra mãe."
(Já terminou!)		 "Eu estava falando com a mãe."
(Estava no meio da ação)
PRACTICE TIME!
Mostre que você é dois super-heróis ao mesmo tempo! ⚡
1. They ___ (watch) a movie at 8 p.m. (Past Continuous)
2. ___ I was riding my bike, I fell. (when ou while?)
3. The lights went out ___ we were watching the movie. (when ou while?)
4. While my brother ___ (look) for his phone, he broke a glass. (Past Continuous)
5. We ___ (have) lunch when the phone rang. (Past Continuous)

🔍 PALAVRAS-CHAVE WH-
PalavraPergunta sobre

⚡ WH- + SIMPLE PAST (com DID)
WH- + DID + SUJEITO + VERBO BASE?
What did you do yesterday? O que você fez ontem?
Where did they go last summer? Onde eles foram no verão passado?
Who did you sit next to on the bus? Ao lado de quem você sentou no ônibus?
🎬 WH- + PAST CONTINUOUS (com WAS/WERE)
WH- + WAS / WERE + SUJEITO + VERBO-ING?
What were your parents doing when you got home? O que seus pais estavam fazendo quando você chegou em casa?
Why were you talking during the class? Por que você estava falando durante a aula?
🏆 SEGREDO DO DETECTIVE — DID ou WAS/WERE?
PerguntaUseExemplo
Ação que aconteceu e terminou DID What did you eat? (verbo base)
Ação que estava em andamento WAS / WERE What were you eating? (verbo+ing)
"Estar" em algum lugar (ser/estar) WAS / WERE Where were you yesterday?
POW! 🎯 Resposta curta: "Yes, I was." / "No, I wasn't." (com was/were) ou "Yes, I did." / "No, I didn't." (com did). Use o mesmo auxiliar da pergunta!
