Q1
SIMPLE PRESENT
My friend Edward _____ paintings and sculptures.
Resposta: B (likes). Edward = he → no Simple Present, he/she/it leva S → likes.
Q2
SIMPLE PRESENT — DO/DOES
_____ your sister play tennis on Sundays?
Resposta: B (Does). Sister = she → pergunta no Simple Present usa DOES + verbo BASE (play, sem S!).
Q3
SHORT ANSWERS
"Do you like to study at Coração Feliz?" → Resposta afirmativa correta:
Resposta: B (Yes, I do). Pergunta com DO → resposta com do. Nunca misture com am/is/are.
Q4
PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Look! She _____ the piano right now.
Resposta: C (is playing). "Right now" → ação acontecendo agora → Present Continuous. She → IS + play+ing.
Q5
IDENTIFICAR PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Marque a frase que está no Present Continuous:
Resposta: B (She is playing very well). Present Continuous = am/is/are + verbo-ing. A) é Simple Past; C) é Simple Present; D) é Past Continuous.
Q6
FORMA CORRETA
Marque a frase correta no Present Continuous:
Resposta: B. Present Continuous precisa de TRÊS partes: sujeito + am/is/are + verbo-ing. Faltando qualquer uma delas, está errado!
Q7
COMPARATIVO CURTO
Sarah's hair is _____ Karen's.
Resposta: B (longer than). "Long" tem 1 sílaba (curto) → comparativo é longer than. NUNCA "more long".
Q8
COMPARATIVO CURTO
Frank is _____ Lisa.
Resposta: A (shorter than). Short = curto → shorter than.
Q9
SUPERLATIVO
Math is _____ subject in school. (na minha opinião!)
Resposta: C (the most difficult). "Difficult" é longo → superlativo = the most difficult. Nunca esqueça do "the"!
Q10
REGRAS — SOMA
Sobre comparativos e superlativos, marque a soma das afirmações CORRETAS:
(01) Comparativos são seguidos por 'than'.
(02) Usamos 'the' antes de superlativos.
(04) "Bigger than" está na forma comparativa.
(08) "The tallest" está na forma comparativa.
(16) "More tall" is the correct comparative of tall.
Resposta: B (07 = 01+02+04). Corretas: 01, 02 e 04. ❌ 08 = "The tallest" é SUPERLATIVO, não comparativo. ❌ 16 = "More tall" está ERRADO; é "taller".
Q11
WAS / WERE
They _____ at school yesterday because it _____ Sunday.
Resposta: A (weren't / was). They = plural → weren't. It (Sunday) = singular → was.
Q12
WAS / WERE — PERGUNTA
_____ you at home last night?
Resposta: B (Were). YOU sempre usa WERE, mesmo quando se refere a 1 só pessoa. "Was you" está SEMPRE errado!
Q13
THERE WAS / THERE WERE
In Peter's old living room: There _____ a beautiful sofa and there _____ four lamps.
Resposta: C (was / were). 1 sofa = singular → was. 4 lamps = plural → were. É só olhar o que vem depois!
Q14
SIMPLE PAST — REGULAR
I _____ in Rome last week and stayed at a beautiful hotel.
Resposta: B (arrived). "Last week" = passado. Arrive termina em E → só adiciona D → arrived.
Q15
SIMPLE PAST — IRREGULAR
This morning my parents and I woke up at 8:00 and _____ a great breakfast.
Resposta: C (had). Have é irregular → passado é had. Cuidado: NÃO existe "haved"!
Q16
SIMPLE PAST — IRREGULAR
Afterwards, I walked a little and _____ many tourists in Rome.
Resposta: C (saw). See é irregular → passado é saw.
Q17
SIMPLE PAST — NEGATIVA
She _____ a cell phone until she was twelve.
Resposta: C (didn't have). Negativa do passado = didn't + verbo BASE. Cuidado: NÃO é "didn't had"!
Q18
SIMPLE PAST — LEITURA
Read the e-mail. All the underlined verbs are in Simple Past, EXCEPT:
Hi Mary! How are you? I miss you so much! I arrived in Rome last week. This morning my parents and I woke up at 8:00 and had a great breakfast. I walked a little and saw many tourists. Come meet us! See you soon!
Resposta: C (Come). "Come meet us" é IMPERATIVO (uma ordem/convite), não Simple Past! Os outros são todos passado: arrive→arrived, wake→woke, have→had.
Q19
PAST CONTINUOUS
While my brother _____ for his phone, he broke a glass.
Resposta: B (was looking). "While" pede Past Continuous. My brother = ele (singular) → was + looking.
Q20
WHEN / WHILE
The lights went out _____ we were watching the movie.
Resposta: C (while). "We were watching" = Past Continuous → combina com while (enquanto).
Q21
FORMA CORRETA
Marque a frase correta:
Resposta: C. A) "they was" ❌ (é were); B) "were eat" ❌ (precisa ser eating); D) "she call" ❌ (precisa ser called).
Q22
PAST CONTINUOUS
We _____ on the beach when somebody stole our camera.
Resposta: C (were sleeping). Estávamos dormindo (ação longa) quando algo aconteceu (ação curta). We → were + sleeping.
Q23
WH-QUESTION
_____ you talking during the class on Thursday?
Resposta: C (Why were). "Talking" = verbo+ing → Past Continuous. YOU sempre usa WERE!
Q24
WH-QUESTION
_____ they finish the project?
Resposta: B (When did). "Finish" está na forma BASE → Simple Past → DID. Estrutura: WH + DID + sujeito + verbo BASE.
Q25
IDENTIFICAR TEMPO VERBAL
Leia este trecho de uma história em quadrinhos:
"Once upon a time there was a house with a man, a dog and a cat. The man was thin. The cat was fat. Then the cat ate my breakfast and the dog chewed my slippers!"
Qual é o tempo verbal principal usado?
Resposta: D (Simple Past). Todos os verbos estão no Simple Past: was (be), ate (eat), chewed (chew). É a Q8 da Prova 2!
